# Cryogenian Writing

Turn an evidence package into a clear scientific argument without inventing
claims, data, or citations.

## Scope

Read `../cryogenian-shared/core/task-scope.md`,
`../cryogenian-shared/core/provenance-and-scale.md`, and, for current claims,
`../cryogenian-shared/core/evidence-freshness.md`.

- `POINT`: write or revise the requested sentence, claim, transition, title, or
  caption first; briefly flag only evidence changes that affect meaning.
- `COMPONENT`: draft the requested section with an argument map.
- `SYSTEM`: build claim architecture, section roles, figures, evidence gaps,
  then produce a coherent manuscript-scale draft.

## Evidence Before Prose

Identify the audience, article type, contribution, claim ceiling, supplied
evidence, and missing support. Distinguish observations, author interpretations,
hypotheses, and unknowns. Use source locators where available. Mark unsupported
placeholders rather than filling them from memory. Refresh current, novelty,
chronology, locality, numerical, causal, or publication-sensitive claims.

## Argument Architecture

Each paragraph has one job: context or observation, tension, inference, and
bounded implication as appropriate. Establish age, setting, preservation, and
proxy validity before environmental reconstruction; establish reconstruction
before mechanism or habitability. Do not treat proxy abundance as independent
corroboration when evidence shares carriers, samples, or age models.

Results report observations and uncertainty. Discussion compares mechanisms,
alternative explanations, scale, and falsifiers. “Habitability” means
environmental potential unless evidence shows biological presence or activity.

## Product And Audit

Return the requested prose plus a compact claim ledger for `COMPONENT` or
`SYSTEM`: claim, evidence, locator, scale, confidence, alternative, and citation
status. Audit terminology, chronology, overclaiming, causal verbs, unsupported
globalization, citation placement, and consistency with figures/tables. Match
the requested journal but do not imitate proprietary text.
