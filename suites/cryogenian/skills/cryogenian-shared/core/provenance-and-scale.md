# Provenance And Scale

Use `OBS`, `CTX`, `INT`, `HYP`, and `UNK` for full analyses. Qualify each with
an evidence-freshness state when it matters. Attach the smallest defensible
scale: time resolution, sample/bed/section/basin/region/global extent,
depositional domain, and preservation state.

Do not count proxies as independent when they share a carrier, sample set,
normalization, age model, or process. Separate surface from deep water, local
from global, concentration from flux, environmental potential from biological
occupation, and temporal association from causality.
