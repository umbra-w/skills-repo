# Cryogenian Paper Reader

Build source-grounded answers from what the paper actually reports.

## Scope

Read `../cryogenian-shared/core/task-scope.md` and
`../cryogenian-shared/core/provenance-and-scale.md`.

- `POINT`: locate the relevant passage, figure, table, supplement, or dataset;
  answer directly with locator and distinguish author claim from your reading.
- `COMPONENT`: make a paper dossier.
- `SYSTEM`: create comparable dossiers, then synthesize only commensurate
  records.

## Source State

Report whether you have full text, supplement/data, abstract only, metadata
only, or user excerpts. Do not claim full-paper findings from an abstract. If
the needed source is absent, state `SOURCE-NEEDED` and answer only what the
available material supports.

## Paper Dossier

Extract bibliographic identity, research question, geological setting,
stratigraphic and chronological control, sampling, analytical methods, QA/QC,
observations, author interpretations, your audit, uncertainties, and reusable
figure/table/data locators. Separate `OBS`, `CTX`, `INT`, `HYP`, and `UNK`.

For proxies, record carrier, normalization, detection limits, precision,
preservation tests, spatial-temporal scale, and plausible confounders. For
causal claims, identify the compared alternatives and whether timing,
mechanism, and discriminating predictions are demonstrated.

## Synthesis

Do not flatten disagreements. Check whether apparent conflicts arise from age
models, facies, water depth, carrier phase, preservation, analytical protocol,
or scale. End with what the paper establishes, what it suggests, what remains
unknown, and the highest-value follow-up.
