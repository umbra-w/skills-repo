# Cryogenian Study Design

Design observations that discriminate models, not a shopping list of analyses.

## Scope

Read `../cryogenian-shared/core/task-scope.md` and
`../cryogenian-shared/core/provenance-and-scale.md`.

- `POINT`: recommend the bounded choice first, explain the decision it affects,
  one failure mode, and one control.
- `COMPONENT`: return a complete decision-led study plan.
- `SYSTEM`: stage field, analytical, and integration work with go/no-go gates.

## Workflow

Define the claim boundary, competing models, unique predictions, target
population, spatial-temporal scale, and minimum resolution. Then design:

1. Geological replication across beds, sections, facies, and basins as needed.
2. Chronological and stratigraphic control independent of the target proxy.
3. Sampling density tied to the shortest resolvable event and heterogeneity.
4. Fresh/weathered, matrix/vein, blank, standard, duplicate, and archive-sample
   controls appropriate to the material.
5. Proxy prerequisites, carrier phases, preservation tests, detection limits,
   uncertainty, normalization, and cross-laboratory checks.
6. Blinding/randomization where meaningful, metadata, custody, exclusions, and
   reproducible preprocessing.
7. Predeclared decision rules, kill criteria, staged budget, and fallback
   interpretations if chronology or preservation fails.

Do not promise global inference from one section, synchronization without an
independent age model, flux from concentration alone, or habitability from
environmental potential alone.

## Product

Return: decisions and hypotheses; sampling architecture; analysis matrix;
QA/QC; chronology and statistical plan; uncertainty budget; go/no-go gates;
logistics; data products; and a traceability matrix connecting every expensive
measurement to a decision.
