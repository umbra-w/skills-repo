# Cryogenian Hypothesis Lab

Protect creative discovery from premature audit, then validate selected ideas.

## Start

Read `../cryogenian-shared/core/task-scope.md` and
`../cryogenian-shared/core/discovery-validation.md`. Keep discovery and
validation visibly separate.

- `POINT`: give the requested alternative, mechanism, prediction, or falsifier
  directly; add only the most decisive caveat.
- `COMPONENT`: generate a discovery portfolio, then validate a shortlist.
- `SYSTEM`: map anomalies across the programme and rank testable models by
  information gain.

## Discovery Space

First restate the phenomenon without embedding its favored explanation.
Generate from anomalies, proxy disagreement, missing mass or flux, reversed
causality, hidden variables, cross-scale discontinuities, preservation bias,
counterfactuals, and cross-domain analogies. Include conservative, mechanism-
recombining, and high-risk ideas. Label `ANOM`, `IDEA`, `HYP`, and
`NOVELTY-UNK`. Lack of precedent or immediate feasibility is not a discovery-
stage rejection criterion.

For each candidate give a causal chain and at least one observable consequence.
Do not fabricate supporting evidence.

## Validation Space

Only after the portfolio exists, choose candidates using explanatory reach and
diversity. For each selected model perform targeted live novelty search,
mechanism and mass-balance accounting, competing-model comparison, unique
predictions, falsifiers, kill tests, feasibility, and information-gain ranking.
Use maturity labels: `speculative`, `mechanistically-formed`,
`literature-compatible`, `test-ready`, or `weakened`.

## Product

Return the anomaly map, unfiltered idea portfolio, selection rationale,
validated hypothesis cards, discriminating experiment matrix, and residual
unknowns. Treat “novel” as `NOVELTY-UNK` until a dated search supports a bounded
novelty statement.
