## Research Focus Supplied by the User

Study Sturtian glaciation iron formations (CIF/BIF) in the Nanhua Basin, South
China. Integrate sedimentology, paleogeography, mercury isotopes, and other
evidence to reconstruct open-water conditions and their control on
atmosphere-land-ocean material cycling, then evaluate deglaciation, marine
environmental change, and early habitability.

## Claim Chain to Test

Treat this as a hypothesis graph, not a preset narrative:

```text
ice and basin configuration
-> local water-surface and circulation state
-> delivery/recycling of Fe, Hg, nutrients, and detritus
-> redox interfaces and mineral/particle scavenging
-> CIF/BIF and Hg sedimentary record
-> deglacial environmental opportunity
-> potential habitability and separately tested biological response
```

Every arrow requires independent timing, process, and preservation evidence.

## Evidence Packages

### Open water

Provide measured logs, photographs, facies descriptions, bed relationships,
paleocurrent/ice-flow data where available, lateral equivalents, and age control.
Do not reduce the case to a single sedimentary structure.

### Iron formation

Provide petrography/mineralogy, primary-versus-secondary phase assessment,
major/trace elements, REE+Y, Fe/S/C isotope data when available, enclosing
lithologies, facies controls, and metamorphic/weathering context.

### Mercury

Provide raw Hg concentration, standards/blanks/uncertainty, `delta202Hg`,
`Delta199Hg`, `Delta201Hg` when measured, normalization variables, TOC/S/Fe/Al/Ti,
host-phase evidence, stratigraphic positions, replicates, and preservation data.

### Cycling and habitability

Provide weathering/runoff, nutrient, redox-depth, productivity, circulation, and
biological/preservation evidence as separate packages. Favorable chemistry is
not evidence of organismal occupation.

## Default Competing Models

1. Persistent or recurrent local open water promoted exchange, mixing, Fe
   oxidation/precipitation, and Hg photochemical/depositional transfer.
2. Seasonal or short-lived ice-margin openings produced the same local signals
   without sustained deglaciation.
3. Restricted-basin stratification and internal recycling controlled Fe/Hg,
   while open-water evidence records only the surface or another facies.
4. Carrier-phase, detrital, diagenetic, or metamorphic changes generated part of
   the geochemical pattern.
5. Age/correlation mismatch juxtaposes records that were not synchronous.

Add or remove models only from supplied evidence.

## Highest-Value Discriminators

- independent age brackets linking diamictite, open-water facies, CIF/BIF, and
  Hg behavior;
- multi-section facies and geochemical replication across paleogeographic positions;
- Hg host phases and preservation, not concentration/isotopes alone;
- separation of surface-water, deep-water, sediment-water, and porewater signals;
- source/flux versus scavenging/dilution mass-balance tests;
- organism-specific habitability and syngenetic biosignature checks.

## Intake Checklist

Ask for the smallest missing item that blocks the current claim:

- target section, formation/member, coordinates or locality ID;
- measured log and sample-height table;
- age constraints and exact dated-horizon relations;
- lithology/facies/petrography/mineralogy;
- analytical methods, raw values, uncertainty, QA/QC;
- crossplots/normalizers and carrier-phase data;
- exact claim, intended spatial-temporal scale, and competing interpretation;
- source papers or data files with claim pointers.

Do not delay a bounded analysis to request every possible item.

## Generalization Boundary

Use this profile only as the anchor. For Marinoan events or other basins, retain
the core workflow but replace local stratigraphy, paleogeography, proxy baselines,
and hypothesis priors with source-grounded information from that system.
