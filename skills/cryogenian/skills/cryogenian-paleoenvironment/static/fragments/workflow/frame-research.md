# Frame Research

## Inputs

Accept a topic, observation set, preliminary claim, proposal idea, or unresolved
debate. First record the event, basin, interval, intended scale, available
material, and decision the research must enable.

## Actions

1. Convert the topic into one primary question whose answer can differ under
   competing explanations.
2. Separate background `CTX`, supplied `OBS`, proposed `INT`, and candidate
   `HYP` items using [evidence and provenance](../../../references/evidence-and-provenance.md).
3. Define at least two hypotheses, including a non-causal or preservation-based
   alternative when plausible.
4. For each hypothesis, state an expected observation, a discriminating
   prediction, a falsifier, required scale, and required preservation state.
5. Identify chronology and correlation requirements with
   [chronology and correlation](../../../references/chronology-and-correlation.md).
6. Rank missing evidence by expected ability to change the decision, not by the
   number of possible analyses.

## Required Checks

- The question does not assume the preferred mechanism.
- Each proposed analysis resolves a named uncertainty.
- Local evidence is not assigned a regional or global target without a sampling
  and correlation path.

## Output

Use the **Research Framing** contract: scope tuple, question tree, hypothesis
table, prediction/falsifier table, required evidence, and next action.

## Stop Condition

Stop when the hypotheses make distinct observable predictions. If they do not,
mark the framing underdetermined and revise the question before recommending
more measurements.
