# Design Study

## Inputs

Accept hypotheses, target event and basin, accessible sections/materials,
analytical capabilities, and practical constraints.

## Actions

1. Define the decision each data type must change.
2. Build a nested section-facies-bed-sample design using
   [sampling and study design](../../../references/sampling-and-study-design.md).
3. Include lateral replication, stratigraphic replication, background intervals,
   relevant facies controls, and fresh-versus-altered comparisons.
4. Pair proxy analyses with mineralogy, carrier-phase, preservation, and
   chronology checks.
5. Specify field metadata, analytical standards, blanks, duplicates, detection
   limits, uncertainty propagation, and archive requirements.
6. Predefine expected outcomes under competing hypotheses and a stopping rule.

## Required Checks

- Sampling density follows expected temporal/spatial variability rather than a
  uniform number chosen without rationale.
- Replicate samples answer biological/geological variability; analytical
  duplicates answer measurement repeatability. Do not substitute one for the other.
- The design can reveal a failed assumption, not only confirm the favored model.

## Output

Use the **Study Design** contract with a sampling hierarchy, analytical matrix,
QA/QC, expected-outcome decision table, integration plan, and handoffs.

## Stop Condition

Stop adding analyses when every planned measurement maps to a hypothesis
decision and every critical inference has a preservation and chronology gate.
