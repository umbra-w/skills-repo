# Output Contract

Choose the smallest artifact that resolves the request. Do not emit every
template by default.

## Out-of-Domain Route

If the request contains no Cryogenian, glacial, geochemical, or
paleoenvironmental reasoning task, do not select a domain workflow. Return only
the boundary decision, name the appropriate companion skill (for example,
`cryogenian-polishing` for domain prose revision), and identify any input needed
for that handoff. Do not manufacture domain relevance.

## Research Framing

1. Scope tuple: event, basin, interval, spatial scale, time resolution.
2. Primary question and bounded subquestions.
3. Competing `HYP` items.
4. Prediction/falsifier table.
5. Required evidence and highest-information next step.

## Evidence Audit

1. Claim under review.
2. Evidence ledger with provenance pointer and scale tuple.
3. Chronology, context, preservation, and proxy-validity gates.
4. Claim-evidence matrix with strength.
5. Conflicts, `UNK` blockers, bounded verdict, and repair actions.

## Proxy Interpretation

1. Measurement and analytical basis.
2. Direct sensitivity and supported scale.
3. Prerequisite gate results.
4. Plausible causes and confounders.
5. Required independent evidence.
6. Maximum defensible conclusion.

## Reconstruction

1. Time slices and spatial/depositional domains.
2. Provenance-labelled observation inventory.
3. Evidence matrix.
4. Environmental states and confidence.
5. Mechanism chain with alternatives.
6. Uncertainty register and discriminating tests.

## Study Design

1. Target hypothesis and decision criterion.
2. Section/facies/sample hierarchy.
3. Analyses, controls, standards, blanks, and replication.
4. Preservation and carrier-phase checks.
5. Expected-outcome decision table.
6. Integration, statistics, archive, and stopping rules.
7. Named specialist handoffs with transferred inputs and affected decisions.

## Argument Review

1. Major claims and exact pointers.
2. Evidence sufficiency and scale match.
3. Overreach or omitted alternatives.
4. Severity and repair action.
5. Decisive additional test.
6. Revised bounded wording when requested.

## Handoff Block

End actionable analyses with a handoff when specialist work is needed:

| Recipient expertise | Inputs transferred | Check requested | Decision affected |
|---|---|---|---|

If the selected workflows include `design-study`, finish with this populated
table. Name the expertise, transferred inputs, requested check, and affected
decision; an analysis list does not replace the handoff.

## Language and Terminology

Respond in Chinese to Chinese requests unless asked otherwise. At first use,
give consistent Chinese-English terminology for important domain terms. Preserve
reported isotope symbols and distinguish `delta202Hg` mass-dependent
fractionation from `Delta199Hg` and `Delta201Hg` mass-independent fractionation.
