# Geochemistry Generalization

The suite has a deep Cryogenian profile but its research method applies across
sedimentary geochemistry, isotope geochemistry, paleoenvironments, weathering,
ocean redox, element cycling, and geobiology.

For work outside the Cryogenian:

1. Keep the scope, evidence freshness, provenance, uncertainty, discovery, and
   validation contracts.
2. Replace Cryogenian-specific assumptions with a task-local domain profile:
   system boundary, materials, reservoirs, carriers, fractionation pathways,
   transport, transformations, sinks, chronology, preservation, and scale.
3. Acquire current primary evidence and method standards for the new system.
4. State which Cryogenian analogies are transferable and which are not.
5. Never use the Nanhua Basin, CIF, Snowball Earth, or Hg-isotope profile as a
   default explanation for another period, basin, material, or isotope system.

Route unrelated analytical-instrument operation, mineral exploration, mantle
petrology, cosmochemistry, or environmental regulation to a specialist when
the suite lacks the needed technical contract. It may still frame the question,
evidence needs, and handoff without pretending domain depth.
