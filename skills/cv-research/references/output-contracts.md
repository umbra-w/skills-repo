# Output Contracts

## Idea brief

```text
Problem and stakes
What is known
Precise gap
Central hypothesis
Competing explanations
Proposed method or study
Falsification plan
Expected contribution
Feasibility and risks
Next decision gate
```

## Experiment plan

```text
Claim-evidence matrix
Datasets and split rationale
Metrics and decision thresholds
Baselines and fairness controls
Main comparison
Ablations and negative controls
Robustness/generalization tests
Statistics and seeds
Leakage audit
Compute budget and run order
Outcome-to-decision mapping
Artifact/logging checklist
```

## Result analysis

```text
Verified observations
Effect sizes and uncertainty
Which hypotheses are supported, contradicted, or unresolved
Alternative explanations remaining
Failure modes and subgroup behavior
Allowed claim wording
Next highest-information experiment
```

## Paper audit

```text
Executive assessment
Claim-evidence ledger
Novelty and closest-work risks
Technical soundness risks
Experimental adequacy
Writing and presentation issues
Reproducibility and submission checks
Prioritized revision plan
```

Keep status labels explicit: `verified`, `author-reported`, `inferred`, `proposed`, `missing`, or `contradicted`.
